Semester Project Plan – Supermarket Startup
It is a supermarket tycoon where you act as a manager and upgrade / maintain your store for as long as you can. You start as a small convenience store and then build into a massive storefront. As the manager you purchase products to stock store shelves that customers purchase. You will be able to upgrade these shelves so that they can hold more product. As you progress through the game you will be able to unlock additional product categories and expand the footprint of your store. Employees will be working at the store, but they won’t have any negative traits and only purpose is to be an addition drain on the player’s wallet. There won’t be any negative events that can knock the player off course other than mismanaging their money.
-- Core Mechanics --
There will be a build system for the player to build out the general section of their store. There will be an employee system where the player can hire and fire employees to work at their store. The game should have a day counter where every day in game is 10 real life minutes. The player cannot change how the days pass and must submit to the flow of time. 
-- Employee System --
The number of max checkout employees will be dynamic, tied to the number of checkout lanes that they have placed in the checkout section of the store, making the valid range of employees for this section 0 to number of lanes. The player is allowed to have zero checkout lanes which should stop their ability to sell products in their store and send out a warning to the player that they need at least one checkout employee.
The number of max butcher employees will be 5, making the valid range of employees for this section 0 to 5. When the number of employees that the player has hired reaches 0, the butcher section must be closed and prevent customers from being able to pay for the food that the butcher serves. The game then serves a notice that the butcher is closed due to low staffing.
The number of max bakery employees will be 5, making the valid range of employees for this section 0 to 5. When the number of employees that the player has hired reaches 0, the bakery section must be closed and prevent customers from being able to pay for the food that the bakery serves. The game then serves a notice that the bakery is closed due to low staffing.
The number of max deli employees will be 5, making the valid range of employees for this section 0 to 5. When the number of employees that the player has hired reaches 0, the deli section must be closed and prevent customers from being able to pay for the food that the deli serves. The game then serves a notice that the bakery is closed due to low staffing.
The storage section of the store will have two employee types. The first are unloaders, which take product from the trucks, to the storage racks inside the storage sections. The max number of unloaders the player can have is 6, which make the valid number of unloaders 0 to 6. When the number of unloaders the player has is 0, trucks must take up a stall inside the storage area and hold back other deliveries that the player may have placed. The game then serves a notice to the player that trucks aren’t getting unloaded due to having no unloaders.
The other type of employees in the storage section will be stockers. These employees stock the shelves inside the general and alcohol sections of the store. The max number of employees the player can have is 8, which make the valid range of employees 0 to 8. When the number of stockers reaches 0, the shelves in the general and alcohol sections of the store must stop being stocked with product even if there is product in the storage section of the store. The game then serves a notice to the player that the shelves will not be stocked due to having no stockers.
The daily wage of all of these employees must be inscribed values that can be changed inside the Unity Editor. 
-- Store Sections --
The store will be split into six sections that the player can unlock through progression. The sections are the general area, storage, butcher, bakery, deli, checkout, and alcohol. The general section is where the player can place shelves, coolers, and freezers. The player can modify the layout of this section to fit whatever needs they want. The storage, butcher, bakery, deli, checkout, and alcohol sections are preset and the player cannot modify the layout of these areas. They can however upgrade the items inside these sections to be able to hold more product and improve employee efficiency.
The checkout section of the store is where the customers checkout with the products that they have decided to purchase. The checkout lanes in this section will be 4x3 and have 5 levels, level 1 to level 5, that the player can pay to upgrade. Upgrading these will improve checkout efficiency making the store serves more customers per day. The player starts with 1 StoreCell then can pay for more space to expand the number of checkout lanes that they can have.
The general section of the store is where the shelves, coolers, and freezers are located. The player can modify this section of the store however they see fit. Shelves will be 1x4x1, coolers will have two types 2x4x1 and 2x4x0.5; freezers will be 2x4x1 and 2x4x0.5. All three types can be upgraded from level 1 to level 5. Upgrading these items will improve the number of items they can hold.
The alcohol section of the store is where the liquor products are stored. Shelves will be 1x4x1 and have 5 levels, level 1 to level 5. The player must obtain a liquor license in order to purchase this section. The process of obtaining a liquor license is a $x fee first to file background check paperwork. The response for this should take 3 to 10 in game business days. There is a chance that this can fail and the player can lose $x and be force to refile for the license. The second stage is a $y fee to file more paperwork. The response for this stage should take 5 to 20 in game business days. There is a chance that this stage can also fail and if so the player will still lose $y and have to restart the entire liquor license process from the beginning. Only after obtaining the license will they be able to purchase the alcohol section of the store.
The Deli section of the store is where sandwiches and prepared products are sold. The customers can places orders with the deli employees and the employees will make it to order. The equipment layout of this section cannot be modified by the player, however they can upgrade the various equipment that they have as the equipment will have 5 levels, level 1 to level 5. Upgrading the equipment will improve employee efficiency.
The bakery section of the store is where fresh bread, cake, cupcakes, cookies and doughnuts are sold. The customers can grab whatever food is on the shelves that the bakery employees put there. They can only stock the shelves with food that they made with the various equipment inside the bakery section. The equipment will have 5 levels, level 1 to level 5. The player cannot modify the layout of this section.
The butcher section of the store is where customers can request custom orders of products. They can prepare different cuts of meat and seafood. The equipment layout of this section cannot be modified by the player. The equipment will also have 5 levels, level 1 to level 5.
The storage section of the store is where deliveries enter the store and where all products end up before the stockers put them on the shelves. This section will have 3 loading zones that the player can upgrade from level 1 to level 5. Upgrading these loading zones will improve the efficiency of the unloaders. The player should start with just one loading zone and purchase more as they progress. The layout of this section cannot be modified by the player, but they can upgrade the large shelves from level 1 to level 5. Upgrading these shelves only improves their capacity and doesn’t effect the employees in any way. The size of these large shelves will be 2x4x3.
-- Shelves, Coolers, Freezers --
All attributes of the shelves, coolers, and freezers should be modifiable in the Unity Editor. All of these items will have a capacity attribute that is the number of items that they can hold. The material of these items should change to signify which level it is.
-- Equipment --
The equipment in the employee sections of the store will have a efficiency attribute and yield attribute. The efficiency attribute will be how fast an employee can use it to turn a raw product into the finished product. The yield attribute will be the number of finished product that is returned. These attributes must be modifiable inside the Unity Editor.
-- UI --
The button to enter build mode will be in the bottom right corner. The button to modify employee hires will be in the bottom left corner. In the top left corner there should be a tab that the player can glance at to see which day they are on and the rate at which they are making money in $ per real life minute. The rate should be the amount of money they are making off of customers minus the amount they are paying their employees. The date should be formatted month/day/year. In the top right corner there is the current dollar amount of money that the player has. Above the build button is a button that takes the player into the order manager where they can purchase products that are needed for their store. The game should suggest the number of products that they should purchase to keep up with demand. These numbers should be slightly higher than what is actually needed. The player ultimately has final say over what is purchased.
